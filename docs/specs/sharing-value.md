# What sharing gets you, where you can see it

Draft, from the designer's look for a "Better together" screenshot (site-screenshots, 11-19).

**Why:** No screen shows a visitor what sharing gets them without our explaining it. The best shot today is Ask's mote
farm at 50, where the player's own logs can't rank a place and players who share do. It works only with the caption:
- **Community numbers look like your own.** Same rows, same grey, a small "3 players" under the place. A place you've
  never run, answered only by others, reads like one you've played.
- **Sharing gives the sharer nothing extra.** Players who don't share read the same numbers. Nothing tells a sharer
  what their play added, so "Better together" is a request, not an offer.
- **The pool is thin, and it says so in ways that read as failure.** Five players: "2 players", "none has enough
  players since the patch yet", rates from before the patch "run lower".
- **Among few sharers, you are part of "players who share" for anyone else.** A sharer's own Ask leaves their play out
  since 0.10.3, but with 5 sharers every number rests on two or three of the others, and many are hidden for it.

**Players:** not sharing (what would they get?); sharing among few (what did they add?); new, log just on (where
others' numbers are all they have).

```text
  4 places in your answers come only from other players.   Every place they know ->
```

## Proposal

1. In every answer, mark a row or route step that has none of your play: "Never farmed by you" / "Never leveled here
   by you", and say in the answer's first line how many places players who share added.
2. A sharer's card on Home: what their shared play is in, and how much of their answers came from others. Counted on
   the PC from what it uploaded and the community file; nothing new sent.
3. Thin pool: say what exists ("11 places, 5 players") rather than what doesn't, and say when a number will appear.

**Tier:** standard for 1 and 3 (Ask, Home, the engine's answers). 2 is risky if it needs anything from the server.
**Answered (11-19):** sharing stays free to read; the card thanks the sharer. The question was: does sharing stay free to read (the card thanks the sharer), or do some
numbers need you to share (give to get)? The designer's pick: free to read, with the card. Gating would empty Ask for
the new player, the one who needs it most.
