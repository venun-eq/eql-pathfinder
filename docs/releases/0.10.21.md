Relevel race figured by the engine; The farm rate counts your farming; Steadier crawl timing; Groundwork for a new Campaign planner; Class roles for the new planner (beta).

The relevel race on Home and Relevels is now worked out by the same engine that works out the rest of your numbers. It looks and reads the same.

- Your best and typical relevels, the middle band, the splits and "ahead of / behind your best" come from the engine, and are tested against hand-worked relevels.
- No re-read, and nothing to do.

The farm rate now comes from your crawls at 50 at the difficulties you farm for motes: D3 and D4, unless you change
them in Settings → How you play.

- A quick D0 run at 50 no longer drags it down. Ask's Count motes too and Upgrades' hours now price motes at what you
  really farm.
- Ask, Upgrades, Settings and Home's farming card say which difficulties it's from, and say so when none of your
  crawls at 50 were at them.
- The 11-10 patch starts when the servers came back up, wherever you play, not at a fixed hour of your clock.

Behind the scenes, the app now tells when a crawl has gone quiet by the same clock everywhere.

- No change while you play.

Behind the scenes, Plan → Campaign can now keep a second plan beside yours for a new planner we're building.

- You won't see any change in this version: Campaign shows and saves your plan as before.
- When the new planner arrives for beta players, your plan will come across as it is, and your old plan will stay as you left it.
- No re-read.

Beta players can try a new Campaign planner that plans trios that hold up where you level, with carries you choose.

- Turn it on in Plan → Campaign → More options, "Try the new planner (beta)". Your plan comes with you, and turning it off gives back your old plan as you left it.
- A new Class roles tab lists your carries, best first (it starts with MAG, NEC, ENC and RNG). Move, add or remove them, and the plan follows at once.
- Where you level at a difficulty you farm for motes, each trio can hold up: an enchanter or bard, or two of a tank, heals, a slow and crowd control. Shaman and enchanter never share a trio, and haste goes to the classes that hit with weapons.
- None of this adds a run or XP to level: it only chooses between plans that cost the same.
- Only beta players see it (Settings → Join the beta). Everyone else's Campaign is as before.
- No re-read.
- No re-read.
