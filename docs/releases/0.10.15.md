History's Visits tab; One usual for each kind of play; Kills at level 50 now count.

History's Crawls tab is now Visits, because it holds every instance you were in, raids included, and it says how long each took.

- Pick a kind of visit along the top: Leveling, Farming, Grouped, Raids or Not judged, each with how many there are. It replaces "Difficulty instances only".
- A new Time column shows each visit from zoning in to zoning out. Hover it to see where the minutes went: active, idle, after it completed and outside the instance. They add up to the whole.
- A raid's Done tick now means its boss died.

Every tick, colour and word on the overlay now comes from the same "usual" History uses.

- The overlay's ticks and bar colours all come from your earlier visits at the place, within 2 levels, with a rate. No usual yet means no ticks, and a raid shows values only.
- Farming is judged against your visits within 2 levels, so a relevel farming D4 at 40 is compared with D4 at 38-42. Some old History verdicts change because of this, mainly D4 farming on a relevel.
- An earlier visit counts toward a usual only if it has a rate (a finished crawl, or 8 kills).
- Ask ranks places only from visits with a rate, and says how many it left out. Its hours, and Home's hours to go, are marked as estimates.
- The overlay's NEXT header says "estimated", and Home, Ask and the farm rate's (i) call the middle of your farming crawls at 50 "typical" ("Your typical farm at 50 since the patch"); "usual" is only the verdict's baseline.

Kills at level 50 now count, and every level counts a kill the same way: by its slain line.

- At 50, Kills, kills a minute, Kill s and idle fill in on History → Visits and in the overlay. Idle is now taken out at 50 too, so time standing in a crawl no longer counts as active: T5-eq an hour reads higher, and so do your usuals and the farm rate Ask and Upgrades use.
- A visit at 50 with under 8 kills, not finished, now reads "too short" like any other, and "Hide visits too short to judge" hides it (0.10.14 kept them visible). The "kills at level 50 aren't counted yet" note is gone.
- Below 50, kills of mobs that give no XP now count, and quest and task hand-ins no longer do. Kill counts and kills a minute change at every level. XP per kill leaves out kills whose XP the log doesn't show.
- Your logs are read again once to count this way.
- If you share, your history is sent again once, over a day or so, so the community numbers count it the new way. The community numbers settle as players update.
- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
