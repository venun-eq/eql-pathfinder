Which difficulty answers the zone you pick; Pathfinder follows your loadout; Your combos (beta).

Ask → Which difficulty shows the players who share for the zone you picked, not for another one.
- Pick a zone and both parts answer it: your crawls on top, the players who share below. Where they haven't played two difficulties there yet, it says so, in place of the numbers for some other zone.
- A zone only the players who share have played now shows that zone, with a line saying your logs don't have it yet.

Pathfinder now knows which loadout you're on, and follows you when you swap.

- Swap to a loadout you've used before and Home, the title bar, the overlay and the fight meter switch to its classes, race and level within seconds, without a /who.
- On a loadout Pathfinder hasn't met yet, or after you change a class, it says so ("loadout 6 not known yet") instead of judging you by the loadout you left. One /who and it remembers that loadout from then on.
- A reincarnation keeps the loadout and its classes, and drops its level to 1.
- Crawls in History keep the loadout they were played on; the Trio tooltip says when it came from your last /who on that loadout, or that the loadout wasn't known.
- The first start re-reads your logs once (a few minutes), so each crawl and fight is read with this loadout state.
- Help has a new "Your loadouts and /who" section. Nothing new is sent anywhere: the loadout number stays on your PC.

Campaign has a new Your combos card, for trios and pairs you love: write one once and every rebuild keeps it. It shows with Join the beta and the new planner on.

- Say which classes play together or apart, or a trio to play once, twice or three times, as a Must or a Prefer. A Must is held above the cheapest path, at the price shown before you add it. A Prefer is kept wherever it costs nothing.
- A combo that costs nothing says nothing: its row shows a price only when it costs runs, XP to level, or a class rank later.
- Set a trio to Unlock now to play its runs back to back. Add a combo shows what it does, and what it would cost as a Must, before you save it.
- When a combo asks more of a class than its goal, the goal tile says so and offers to remove the goal or raise it to 3/3.
- A loadout's menu has Make this a combo. Pinned loadouts you bring across from the old planner become Must combos.
- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
