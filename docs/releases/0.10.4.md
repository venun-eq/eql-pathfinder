Bonus-XP play is shared, marked, and left out of the community numbers; Set your counts from Home, Campaign and Ask; Pages say where their numbers come from; Tidier wording on Home and Campaign; Home waits for a fair session rate; Test copies keep their settings; Save Void-Touched Potentials for +9 → +10; A steadier start after reading your logs; Too few farming crawls, not none; (i) buttons explain the numbers; A rare refresh error with the community numbers; Ask's farm answer says how it counts fragments.

If you share, crawls and hunts played during a bonus-XP event are shared again, marked as bonus play.

- The community numbers leave marked play out, the same way your own Ask does, so a bonus weekend can't make a place look faster than it is.
- Only bonus play carries the mark. Nothing else you share changes, so nothing else is sent again.
- The privacy page lists the new mark among what's sent.
- No re-read.

If your log hasn't seen you reincarnate, Home, Campaign and Ask now say so, with a Set counts button.

- Turned on /log after you'd already reincarnated? Pathfinder can't see those runs, so it plans as if you had none. Until now only Plan → Roster said so.
- The same banner now shows on Home, on Campaign (your plan and its start page) and on Ask's "Level from A to B". Set counts takes you straight to Roster's counts.
- While it's up, the wording says it assumes no reincarnations yet: "First for Warrior and Cleric and Ranger if you haven't reincarnated before", "48 runs from no reincarnations".
- Once you set your counts, or your log sees a reincarnation, the banner goes and everything reads as before.
- No re-read.

Ask, Upgrades and Settings now say where the farm rate comes from, and Ask's hours say which levels they leave out.

- The farm rate (what an hour of farming at 50 is worth) says whether it's your best farm, your usual, the rate you set, or the default of 50 T5-eq/h when you haven't farmed 3 crawls at 50 since the 11-10 patch. Each place that uses it has a link to change it in Settings.
- Level from A to B shows "for 41 of 49 levels" beside the hours when nobody has numbers for some levels, and names them. Home's route bar labels those levels too.
- Every place with nothing of yours yet points you to Level from A to B, which answers from the players who share, and says how it fills in: keep /log on and run a crawl.
- Roster's reincarnation passives read cleanly: the game's files don't hold the amounts, so they no longer show "by X%".

Pathfinder now recommends taking gear to +9 with motes and fragments and saving Void-Touched Potentials for +9 → +10.

- A VTP adds one whole tier, and +9 → +10 is the costliest tier: 512 points, about 1,640 Major motes' worth. That's where a VTP saves the most.
- So a fragment's best use is now +8 → +9, condensed to Ascendant. "Not sure: recommended" in Settings → What's a fragment worth? moves from 4.5× to 3.56× a same-tier mote, and Loot, Home and Ask count fragments that way. A value you picked yourself stays.
- Upgrades spends fragments at +8 → +9 when a VTP does +9 → +10, instead of holding them back for a tier the VTP covers. It notes when a plan uses a VTP below +10.
- No re-read.
