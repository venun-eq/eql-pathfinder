D0 (Normal) crawls are tracked now. They enter under the plain zone name, so Pathfinder used to miss them and count them as open-world hunting. The crawl list, Ask, your relevels and the fight meter (Crawl, not Zone) all pick them up, including past ones from your logs. The first start re-reads your logs once (a few minutes) and the fight meter re-reads its fights (about 15 seconds).
