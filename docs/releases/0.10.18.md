The overlay's next-place ranking tested; Leaving a D0 dungeon by a zone line no longer cuts the visit short; Numbers follow your settings and your loot at once; Scrub makes a safe test log; Behind the scenes: Ask's comparison with players who share and the reincarnation XP penalty now have tests.

Nothing you read changes; the code that picks the places the overlay offers between crawls now stands on its own so it can be tested case by case.

- The NEXT card offers the same places, rates and "again" as before.

A dungeon you walk out of and back into is one visit again, and your 1-point hits count.

- Out of a dungeon at difficulty 0 by a zone line (Nagafen's Lair to Solusek's Eye, say) and back in: the whole stretch is one visit, with its real length, kills and motes. Before, the second half could be missing from History, and motes you looted after were credited to the visit you left.
- Walking out into the open world of the same name and playing there no longer adds kills, motes or deaths to the visit you left.
- The fight meter reads those returns the same way.
- Hits for 1 point of damage are read, so your damage totals, your pet's and the damage you take are a little higher.
- Your logs are read again once to apply this.

Some numbers could lag behind a change until a minute or an hour passed. They now move at once.

- The farming verdict and the NEXT card follow a drop the moment it lands.
- Changing what a fragment is worth, the level cap or the farm rate updates your farm rate, Ask and your journeys straight away.

The command-line `scrub` now writes a log that is safe to share.

- Your character's name is replaced and every time is moved so the log starts at noon, so an excerpt shows what you played and not who or when.
- Nothing changes in the app itself.

Behind the scenes: Ask's comparison with players who share and the reincarnation XP penalty now have tests. Nothing you read changes. No re-read.

- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
