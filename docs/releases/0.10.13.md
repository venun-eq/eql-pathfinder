Verdicts against your earlier visits; Fairer pace band after an empty chest; Crawls too low-level to refund leave your usual alone.

A visit is now judged only against the visits you played before it.

- In History → Crawls, a visit's "on pace", "% slow" or "% more T5-eq/h" no longer changes as you keep playing. It's
  measured against what you'd done up to then.
- A place's first few visits read "No usual yet", because there was nothing to judge them by.
- The visit you're in now reads as before on Home and the overlay, since nothing comes after it. Today's earlier
  stops on Home are judged the same way as History.

A crawl whose chest held no motes no longer throws off the pace band for its place.

- If a crawl was too low-level to earn motes, your other visits there still set how much "usual" can vary.
- No re-read.

A crawl you finished when its opponents were too low-level to earn a refund no longer counts toward your usual T5-eq an hour at that place.

- Those crawls give a chest of gear and no motes, so they said nothing about how well you farm the place.
- The visit itself still shows in History and is still judged.
