Rebuild keeps your runs on screen; Campaign shows the trios you played; Races with no goal (beta); Your usual on the overlay in D3 and D4; Levels/h stays on the overlay in D3 and D4; What you want first (beta).

Plan → Campaign: Rebuild the rest no longer leaves your runs blank when the plan was already the cheapest.

- Each run's gold 3/3s and Completionist rank, its dots and its XP to level come back as soon as the rebuild is done,
  even when it changes nothing. Before, they stayed hidden under "updating…" until you changed a combo.

Plan → Campaign: "runs played before this plan" now lists the trio and race you actually leveled.

- Before, a run showed the classes the game reset together, which can differ from the trio you then played (when you
  reset several loadouts in one go and picked trios afterwards). It now matches History → Relevels, and each run's ranks
  read from the trio shown.
- A reincarnation you haven't played yet, or one the log can't tie to a relevel, still shows the classes the game reset.
- When you reset several loadouts in one go, the runs are numbered in the order you played them, not the order the game
  reset them, and the ranks (a class's 1/3, 2/3, Completionist) land on the run that earned them.

In the new planner (beta), a race you haven't set a goal for can take any run where that costs the least XP to level.

The overlay compares a D3 or D4 crawl you're in with the crawls you finished there.

- It said "No usual here yet" in a place where you had finished plenty of crawls: a crawl still going was compared only with crawls you'd left unfinished.
- The verdict, the tick on T5-eq/h and the note now show while you play, as History shows them once the crawl is over.

Leveling through D3 or D4, the overlay keeps your Levels/h.

- A D3 or D4 crawl below the level cap shows Levels/h under T5-eq/h, which still gives the verdict. Compact shows both, and so does the card when the crawl is done.
- With no usual yet, the note counts your earlier visits toward one: "No usual here yet: 1 of 3 earlier visits like this one at levels 18–22."
- The leveling note for a place with no usual yet now shows on the overlay.

In the new planner (beta), Campaign has a new card, What you want first, for the classes and races you want most.

- Rank a class or a race with one click. Each row has its own pips (its goal) and Spread out | Unlock now, with the price of each in runs and XP to level.
- Drag a row's number, or use its ⋯ menu, to put the ones you want soonest on top. Races you don't rank stay "don't care": Pathfinder uses them where that costs the least XP to level.
- Every race 3/3 ranks all 16 races, yours first and then the fewest ranks first. On the start page, Everything 3/3 does the same.
- The Race passives tile now shows the races you ranked. The pip grid for races is gone, and so is "Earn these first" in More options: both are in the new card.
- When your old plan comes across, a new section says how Earn these first and your race goals became ranked lists.
- No re-read.
