Home follows your loadout; Campaign counts a run done at the cap.

Home now shows the loadout you're on, not the first relevel you left open.

- At the level cap, Home opens on Farming, every time. Next relevel is one click away.
- Below the cap, Home shows a relevel only when your trio and race match it. After a swap it says "Type /who in game" until the log names your loadout.
- Relevels you've left open show as "Parked relevels" under your trio, and Up next lists a Finish step for each.
- A relevel keeps its number (relevel 7 stays 7) whichever one you finish first.

Campaign no longer calls a run done the moment you reincarnate.

- A run is done when its loadout is back at the level cap. Until then it reads "leveling", with its level out of the cap and the hours to go, the same numbers Home shows.
- You can have several runs leveling at once, say when you reincarnate a parked loadout to spend a crawl token. The big number is the earliest run still leveling, with the next run to reincarnate underneath.
- The runs list gets a "leveling" tag and a half-filled dot for each of them, and its header counts how many are leveling.
- A run you reincarnated before you built the plan, and are still leveling, counts too: it keeps its place in the run numbers and gets a row of its own.
- A run you reincarnated but haven't played yet reads "leveling · not played yet" with its level, not "done".
- No re-read.
