Counts beside a number count the visits behind it; The overlay's bars say what the verdict says.

Every count beside a number now counts the visits behind that number. Crawls with no rate yet (under 8 kills and not
finished) were counted in "your typical farm (the middle of your 14 farming crawls)" and in Ask's mote farm list
though they weren't in the rate; now they're left out of both, and Ask's "Left out" line counts them at 50 too. The
farm rate starts once 3 crawls have a rate. The numbers from players who share are rebuilt the same way. No re-read.

The overlay's bars now take their colour from the verdict beside them, so a bar can no longer be green while the verdict says slow.

- Kill speed is green or red by the same call as the word in the header, and XP per kill by the XP call in the note under it. Red means below your usual by more than a normal run's swing.
- Kill speed shows the number the verdict judged, and the note and the done card quote that same number ("Slow kills: 3.1 a minute").
- Levels/h and T5-eq/h while you level, and Kills/min while you farm, stay grey: no verdict judges them. Their tick still shows your usual.
- The amber shade is gone from the bars. Amber now only means the place: Fading or a Slower place.
- Farming's T5-eq/h bar, in a crawl and on the done card, is green for more or usual and red for less.
- Help explains it under "Am I going at a proper speed?".
- No re-read.
