One cost per level; XP at the cap isn't levels; Community numbers follow your rules; Verdicts kept apart from the tracker; Loot's chest share counts fragments properly; Level 50 kills counted right.

The cost of a level is now worked out one way everywhere.

- The overlay's NEXT, Home and the Ask page give the same cost for the same place.
- A place that levels too slowly to price shows no cost, instead of a huge one.

At level 50, kills no longer add made-up levels to History and the overlay.

- A visit at 50 has no levels gained or Lv/h (the cells are empty), because the XP bar fills there but no level follows. A visit that reaches 50 counts the kills before the ding only.
- XP per kill is worked out one way everywhere, over the kills whose XP your log shows, and never includes a crawl's completion XP.
- The overlay's "levels/h = kills/min × the XP a kill gives" line now adds up.
- Your logs are read again once to apply this.

The community numbers are now worked out by the same rules as your own.

- Damage per second in the community numbers counts active time, like yours.
- Short unfinished visits (under 8 kills) no longer set a place's pace in the community numbers, as in your own Ask.
- Kills at the level cap add no XP to a community visit, as in your own play.

Nothing you read changes; the code that works out each visit's verdict now stands on its own so it can be tested case by case.

- History's kinds, usuals and stops read as before.

On Loot, "% from Reward Chests" now counts the fragments a chest gave at their worth, as the T5-eq total already did. It read too low before.

History no longer counts kills at level 50 as kills that gave XP.

- At 50, XP per kill and levels an hour from kills are left out instead of showing 0.
- The kill that takes you to 50 counts the XP it gave, the same way every time.
- Your logs are read once more to apply this.
- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
