Upgrades: the Item box narrows your gear as you type, and Enter picks the top match.

- Type any part of a name ("ring", or two words in any order); the list shows only the gear that matches, with what you typed highlighted.
- Up and Down move through the list, Enter or a click picks one (and sets the tier it's at now), Escape closes it.
- No re-read.
