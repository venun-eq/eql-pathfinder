New: Plan → Campaign plans every reincarnation you mean to do, and updates now install themselves.

- Campaign: pick the passive rank you want (up to 3) for each class and race, or a ready-made goal, and it lays out the loadouts that get there: a race and three classes, run 1 to 3 times. Your goals come first, ranks stay even (the cheapest XP), and heals and a carry go in as many trios as it can.
- It shows the next run, runs to go, hours of relevels and when Completionist goes up. Reincarnations your log sees tick runs off; "Mark a run done" covers ones from while your log was off. Pin the loadouts you've settled on and "Rebuild the rest" plans around them.
- Updates install by themselves once the game has been closed a while and Pathfinder is in the background, and it comes back the way it was (in the tray or minimized).
- Sharing's daily upload limit (a long history goes up over a few days) shows as a note in Settings → Sharing instead of an error.
- The reincarnation penalty reads "+20% XP to level": each level needs that much more XP; mobs give the same.
- No re-read.
