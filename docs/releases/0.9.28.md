Pathfinder now sends anonymous usage counts (pages opened, Ask questions, version, crashes). See exactly what's sent, or switch it off, in Settings.

- What's counted: which pages you open, which Ask questions (and whether the shared numbers could answer them), how long Pathfinder runs with and without the game, setup steps, how long reading your logs takes, your app and Windows versions, and errors (the type and where in Pathfinder, never the message).
- Sent once a day under a random id of its own, not your sharing id. Never your logs, character, play or file paths, and the server keeps no address.
- Settings > Character and data: "Send anonymous usage counts" switches it off and deletes what your copy sent; "See exactly what's sent" shows today's summary.
