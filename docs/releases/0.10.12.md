Pathfinder now judges each visit by what you actually did there, so a D4 crawl is measured in motes and a D1 crawl in levels.

- D3 and D4 are judged by T5-eq per hour against your usual at that place, with "% more", "% less" or "Usual T5-eq/h" in History → Crawls. The rest is leveling, judged by kill speed. A raid shows whether you killed its boss.
- A place only counts as "levels faster" when it is at the same difficulty, so D4 no longer reads as a "slower place" than D0. The "Slower place" label is gone.
- Grouped play, instances you never finished and play at 50 are judged by T5-eq per hour too.
- Home has no Fastest or Counting motes switch any more. It reads what you're doing from your play and says so under the route; Ask is the one place you tell it what you're after.
- Settings → How you play picks which difficulties you farm for motes (D3 and D4 to start). The welcome tour no longer asks for a goal.
- Pathfinder reads your logs once more, so earlier raids show whether you killed the boss.
- Home's place-by-place card judges each stop as a whole (one place at one difficulty), and its XP tile reads in T5-eq/h on a farmed visit.
- The crawl overlay leads with T5-eq per hour while you farm and levels per hour while you level.
- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
