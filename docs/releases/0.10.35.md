Ready for Kunark's level 55; Home mid-relevel; Help links every setting it names.

When Kunark raises the level cap to 55 on 12-08, Pathfinder moves with it: a character at 50 is leveling, not farming at the cap.

- Home, Campaign and Roster count levels out of 55 from 12-08. A relevel you finished at 50 stays finished, and a character at 50 on 12-08 reads as leveling towards 55.
- Ask has no route for levels 51 to 55 until crawls there are played, so it says "No route past 50 yet" (or past the highest level it has a crawl for) instead of guessing, and hours stop at the highest level your finished relevels reached ("about 6.2 h to 50"). Ask's ranges and steppers go up to 55.
- Levels 51 to 55 need their usual XP: a reincarnation only adds XP to levels your class or race had already reached. Home shows "No extra XP to level past 50", and the overlay and Ask's times read the XP of the level you are at.
- Crawls at 50 before 12-08 still count as farming at the cap. Crawls at 50 after it are leveling.
- Pathfinder re-reads your logs once (a few minutes) so play since 12-08 is counted at the new cap.

Home keeps to your real level while you relevel.

- A relevel you have reincarnated into but not played yet now shows as "Leveling now" with its relevel number, trio, race, level and its XP to level, instead of a bare "Leveling" card.
- Parked relevels show whole levels ("47 / 50"), not "47.12 / 50".
- When you farm at the cap, the Farming card's session no longer counts a relevel crawl you ran in the same place on the way up.

Help now takes you to the setting it talks about, and a few lines that pointed at things that weren't there are fixed.

- Every mention of a setting in Help is a link: Settings → Sharing, Counting (the fragment value and the farm rate), How you play, Overlays → Fight meter, and Plan → Roster. A click opens the page and flashes the spot.
- The Motes card no longer says "below": it links where you pick the fragment value and the card that says which to pick.
- Help no longer names Home's old Fastest | Counting motes switch; it says how Ask's route is chosen.
- The pace chips read "Fading · Kedge Keep levels faster" and "Kedge Keep levels faster", as on Home and History.
- The XP per kill table wraps at the usual window width instead of hiding its last row behind a scroll bar.
- A farm number under 1 reads "0.3", or "under 0.1", never "0": in the farm line on Settings → Counting, on Ask's Every place and in Plan → Roster's counts.
- "Planes below 46" no longer says those visits come from before a rule: the planes always needed 46, and such a visit is a loadout you swapped to without a /who. Ask leaves it out.
- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
