Community numbers count fragments your way; One copy writes your history; When you share, Ask compares you with the others, not with yourself; Sharing sends less; Campaign counts the runs you've already played; Kills during bonus-XP events count.

Ask's community numbers now count fragments at your own fragment value, and are worked out from what players share rather than from each copy's own sums.

- The community file has motes and fragments apart, so the community's T5-eq per hour uses your Settings → "What's a fragment worth?", the same as your own numbers.
- Sharing sends two more facts with each crawl: when you stepped out of it and came back, and when it ended, as seconds into the crawl like every kill. Your shared crawls go again once, with them.
- The community numbers are now worked out from the shared facts every time they're built, so a fix to how Pathfinder counts levels or active time reaches everyone's crawls, not just the ones shared since.

Pathfinder's history can no longer be written by two copies at once.

- If a second copy of Pathfinder (or a developer tool) opens your history while Pathfinder is running, it now stops with a message instead of following your log too. Two at once recorded some kills twice.

When you share, Ask now leaves your own play out of the numbers of the players who share.

- With only a few sharers, your own runs were part of most of their numbers, often half of one. So "players who share" partly meant you, "You against players who share" made your gaps look smaller, and the route counted your times twice.
- Now every number is theirs alone: the route, each leg's time beside yours, the comparison, mote farms, difficulties and damage, and the overlay's "Players here".
- Where a number rested on you and one other player, there's no number for you any more, since what's left would be one player's play. Where this answer comes from names those places.
- Nothing changes if you don't share.
- No re-read.

Sharing only sends a crawl again when what happened in it changes, so your new crawls stop waiting behind re-sends.

- Until now an update that changed how Pathfinder counts sent your whole history again. Two in a day hit the server's daily upload limit, and new crawls waited until the next day. Now the community numbers are worked out from the facts on the server, so those updates send nothing.
- Your history goes up once more with this update: it now includes when you stepped out of each crawl.

Campaign now counts the runs you've already played: with 6 behind you, your next run is "Run 7 of 48", not "Run 1 of 42".

- Your runs starts with the runs you played before the plan: each one's race, trio, day and what it earned, locked. Runs your ranks hold that the log never saw (counts you entered on Roster) are counted too.
- Played runs can't be changed. A loadout you've played can't be moved, removed or swapped. If you've played part of one, Swap… splits it first: the played runs stay, and only the runs still to play change.
- Home's "Up next" and every "at run N" in Campaign count the same way.
- No re-read.

Kills during a bonus-XP event now count. Pathfinder missed the game's "(with a bonus)" XP lines, so a crawl played during an event showed 0 kills and 0 levels gained.

- History shows those crawls as they happened: their kills, levels and levels an hour.
- Ask and the pace checks leave play during a bonus event out, since a bonus makes a place look faster than it is. Ask says how many visits it left out.
- Play during a bonus event isn't shared for now, so it can't make the community's numbers look faster either.
- Pathfinder re-reads your logs once after this update to find the kills it missed.
- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
