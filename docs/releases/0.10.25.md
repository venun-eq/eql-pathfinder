Menus stay open; The new planner for everyone.

Campaign's ⋯ menus no longer close by themselves just after you open them.

- A menu used to close if the page had just shifted to bring a button into view, before you could pick from it. It now closes only when you scroll it away.

The new planner is out of beta. Everyone now plans on Plan → Campaign with Your goals, Classes and Races you want first, Your combos and the new Class roles tab.

- Plans made on the old planner don't carry over and have been removed, sorry for the inconvenience: pick a ready-made goal to start a new one. Your ranks and the runs you've played are untouched.
- A plan you made with the new planner in the beta is still yours.
- If you had an old plan, Campaign tells you once, with a short how-to. Press Got it and it won't come back.
- The "Try the new planner" switch and "Start again from your old plan" are gone, along with the old planner's "Earn these first" and "prefer" toggles.
- No re-read.
