Pace, like for like: am I going at a proper speed? While leveling, the overlay now shows Kill speed (kills a minute against your usual at this place and level, solo or group the same) and XP per kill (against what this place gives at your level and XP penalty). The header says on pace, N% slow or N% fast, judged against one run's normal swing (about 15%) and only once you have 3 runs near that level. The note says why: slow kills, low XP per kill, a clearly faster place (Fading or Slower place), or completion XP still to come. History > Crawls gains Verdict and Kill speed columns. Your numbers are shown as they happened; only the comparison adjusts.
