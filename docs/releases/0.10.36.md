A wrong loadout at login no longer takes your levels; Sharing sends facts only.

Logging in no longer puts your levels on the wrong loadout.

- When you log in, the game's files can still name the loadout you played last session. The app now waits for a /who or your next swap before it takes that loadout as the one you're on, and lets it go when you level in a way it couldn't (a ding to 48 on a loadout already at 50).
- Once your logs are re-read, levels that went to the wrong loadout are back on the one you played, in Home, History and Campaign.
- A loadout whose race and classes changed since the app last saw it (a new trio at level 1) no longer keeps its old classes for the levels before your next /who: those levels go to the classes the /who names.

Sharing sends only what happened in your play, and takes back what Pathfinder no longer counts.

- A shared crawl or hunt no longer carries numbers your copy of Pathfinder worked out from it (levels gained, and how much of its time was idle between kills). The community numbers work those out from the facts, the same way for everyone's play.
- When Pathfinder stops counting something you shared (a crawl a re-read put together differently, say), it deletes it from the server at the next send, so it isn't counted twice. Settings → Sharing may show a few fewer crawls and stretches after that.
- The first send after updating sends everything you've shared once more, in the new form. Nothing is counted twice.
- The privacy page now lists everything that's sent, including the spells you began casting, reincarnations and relevels.
- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
