Pathfinder no longer guesses how many hours a trio will take: Campaign plans your next relevels instead.

- How fast a trio levels depends on what its classes do together, so a trio you haven't played can't be timed from the ones you have. The Next trio page, its "fastest first" list, Check any trio and the hours in Campaign are gone.
- Plan opens on Campaign, and Home's Up next shows your campaign's next run with the XP it needs to level.
- In Campaign → What you want, choose what to prefer between loadouts that cost the same runs and XP: a carry, heals, or both.
- History → Relevels now shows your relevels like-for-like and where the hours went, both from relevels you played.
- Hours that come from play stay: your relevels, the one you're on, Ask's routes and Upgrades' farm time.
- No re-read.
