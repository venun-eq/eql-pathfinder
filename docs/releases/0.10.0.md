Pages fill in again; Campaign plans the cheapest way; Upgrades lists your copies, condenses fragments and draws the path.

Pages fill in again: 0.9.37 could leave Home, Campaign and the other pages empty or out of date.

- Every update from your log stopped on a leftover from the removed Next trio page, before the page was drawn. That's fixed, and a new check catches this kind of mistake before a release.

Campaign now plans the cheapest way to the passive ranks you want, says so with the arithmetic, and the dropdowns are gone.

- The cheapest way: the fewest runs, then the least XP to level, then your classes first (Completionist counts only classes). Every class 3/3 from 1/3 everywhere is 11 runs, where it used to plan 12; from scratch 16, not 18. Classes come in rounds (every class to 1, then 2, then 3), so Completionist comes as soon as it can.
- Click a class's or race's pips for the rank you want. The plan says whether it's the cheapest ("42 race ranks to earn, one a run") and when your classes are done.
- When a plan misses a goal or has runs to spare, it offers fixes, cheapest first, priced in runs and XP.
- Your runs are one list. Swap… shows the cheapest changes that keep every goal, or lets you pick a race or class yourself, with what each choice does. The ⋯ menu sets runs, moves, marks a run done, or removes.
- The run order switch is gone: the plan is already in the cheapest order. Have a plan? "Rebuild the rest" re-plans it this way and keeps pinned loadouts and runs you've played.
- Fixed: opening Help could leave other pages without their data until the next update.

Upgrades: pick an item and every copy you have is listed and merged in, fragments are kept for your highest gear, and the plan is drawn as a path you can follow.

- Your copies: each one from your inventory export, with where it is, its +N and the points already in it. Untick one to leave it out, or add a copy the export missed.
- Fragments condense 2:1 at Constructed Potential since today's patch. Condensed up to a Fragment of Infinite, any fragment is worth most in a +9 item, so the plan keeps them for that and condenses them when it gets there. The "spells to rank" switch is gone.
- Your motes: fragments you condense move up a tier, the way motes do.
- The path: a bar for each copy on one +N scale, coloured by the motes that fill it, then the merges and VTPs.
- What it takes: every mote and fragment the plan uses, then what to farm and condense.
- Step by step: one step per copy with everything that goes into it, then each merge.
- No re-read.
