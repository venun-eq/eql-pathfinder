Sharing is for everyone now (no beta needed): share your play and Ask shows how fast other players level and farm where you haven't been. Charmed pets now count as yours only while the charm holds, so damage and DPS are right for enchanters, necros and bards. The first start re-reads your logs once (a few minutes) and the fight meter re-reads your fights (about 15 seconds).
