Fairer "low XP per kill"; Campaign stays where you scrolled.

"Low XP per kill" now compares a crawl with your runs at the same levels.

- Your +20% XP to level was counted all along. The mistake was in what a place gives a kill: one line through every level you'd played there read too high at the lowest and highest levels, and called a normal run low. It's now taken from your runs there within 2 levels of yours, each moved to your level and put at your XP penalty.
- The overlay's XP per kill and Levels/h ticks in a crawl use the same number, so they move with it.

Campaign no longer jumps back to a button when its notice goes away.

- Removing a combo: the "Removed" line under Your combos goes after a few seconds, and the page stays where you scrolled it.
- Start over: when "Click again to clear the plan" turns back into "Start over", the page stays where it was.
- No re-read.
