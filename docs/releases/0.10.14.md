One rule for every rate; Pace numbers worked out in one place.

A rate an hour (levels, T5-eq, kills a minute, damage a second) now means the same thing on every page and in the overlay.

- A visit has a rate once it is a finished crawl or has 8 kills. Before that it shows "—" and says when the rate starts, where it used to show a number that swung wildly or a zero.
- A short crawl you finished always has its rate, however short.
- History → Crawls: "Hide visits too short to judge" hides the visits with no rate yet (raids, judged by their boss, and visits at the level cap stay). Finished crawls stay. When it hides every visit, the empty list says how many and why.
- Damage a second in Crawls now divides by your active time like the other rates, so it reads higher when you had idle time. The fight meter still counts only seconds in a fight.
- The overlay and Home's session card say "The rate starts at 8 kills" instead of a time, and a visit with no rate reads "too short" where it used to read "no usual yet".
- No re-read.

Behind the scenes, the kill-speed verdicts, the "levels/h fell about N% a level" lines and Ask's cost per level now come from one place instead of being worked out again on each page.

- Nothing you see should change: History's relevel lines, Home's pace chips and the cost column in Ask's Every place read as before.
- Searching History for a place or trio leaves a relevel's header describing the whole relevel, not only the visits that match. When the search hides some of its visits, the header now says so: "numbers for the whole relevel; your search shows 3 of its 14 visits".
- No re-read.
