Crawls keep the levels of the loadout that played them; Home follows your goal.

After you swap loadouts, a crawl now keeps the levels of the loadout that actually played it.

- The race chart and the splits on Home no longer jump to 50 for a relevel you were still leveling, after your main was swapped in and out.
- A crawl you played at 32 is no longer counted as starting at your main's 50, and one you reincarnated in the middle of no longer ends at 1.
- Your history is read once more to correct crawls already stored, and the shared numbers on Ask leave out crawls whose levels couldn't have happened.

Home now compares your relevels like for like, through the goal you picked.

- A Fastest | Counting motes switch sits beside Ask's route on Home, so you can change your goal without going to Settings.
- Home compares a relevel only with your relevels played mostly at the same difficulty as Ask's route, so a D4 relevel is no longer measured against one played at D0.
- With none of that kind yet, Home says "Nothing to beat at D2 yet" and never falls back to a different kind.
- Your relevels shows each one's difficulty and T5-eq beside its hours, and fades the ones that aren't compared.
- To beat, the pace line, the splits, the race and Farming's "When you're done" all use the same like-for-like best.
- No re-read.
- The first start after this update re-reads your logs once (a few minutes).
