Report a problem from any page; Fragments are worth what your gear makes them; Campaign shows your goals, and re-plans when they change.

Something looks wrong? Press Report on the left, or Ctrl+Shift+R, on the page where you see it.

- It takes a screenshot of Pathfinder's page for you, with your character's name, clock times and folders already hidden. You see it before anything goes, and can leave it out.
- Mark it up: draw a box around what's wrong, or black out anything else you'd rather not send.
- It says which page you were on and your window's size and display scaling, which is often what a layout problem comes down to.
- Help → Your reports lists what you've sent, what's happened to each one, and any reply.

Fragments now count at what they're really worth, and Pathfinder recommends a value if you're not sure.

- Fragments condense 2:1 since the 11-17 patch, so each one is worth most in your gear's top tier: 4.5 motes at +9 → +10, against 2 on a spell.
- Settings → What's a fragment worth? "Not sure: recommended" is the default (4.5× today) and follows Pathfinder's advice if the rules change. If you know how high you take the gear you upgrade with motes, pick that: +10 4.5×, +9 3.56×, +8 2.75×, +7 2.29×, or "+6 or lower, or spells only" 2.0×.
- Loot, Home, Ask and the drop banner count fragments at that value, so places that drop more fragments rank higher.
- If you had the old 2.0 default you're moved to Recommended. A 1.0 or 1.6 you picked yourself stays.

Campaign shows which ranks are your goals, and changing a goal re-plans at once.

- A gold ring marks every rank you want, and the class or race is outlined, even when the plan reaches that rank anyway. Ranks past your goal are dimmed.
- "Every class 3/3" and "Every race 3/3" light up with a tick while they're your goal. Click one again to take it away.
- Change a goal and Pathfinder plans the cheapest way to the new goals straight away, keeping pinned loadouts and runs you've played. Before, the old runs stayed until you rebuilt, so a goal you picked could look like nothing happened.
- A loadout you change by hand is pinned for you, so changing your goals plans the rest around it.
- While a change is on its way, the cheapest-path line and the fixes stay on the page, faded, rather than vanishing and coming back.
- "1 class rank to earn", not "1 class ranks".
- No re-read.
