Ask's route uses your times and the community's together; Log-off warning only when it's certain.

Ask's "Level from A to B" now counts your times and the times of the players who share together, yours counting more the more you've played a place, and shows where you're behind them.

- Where both have a place, its time is yours and theirs together: their number counts as much as one of your visits, so with four visits the time is 80% yours. Before, the route came from your logs alone, with theirs off to the side.
- A place only they have played at those levels joins the route at their time, marked "new to you".
- Each leg shows your time and theirs side by side ("22% less time") and how much of the time is yours.
- You against players who share: every place you've both played at those levels, most time lost first. Where you take clearly longer, it says whether it's kill speed or XP per kill.
- The solo and grouped answers use the players' solo and grouped numbers the same way.
- Fixed: the players' route could say "0 players" when a level at its start was read from the level beside it.
- No re-read.

"Your log is off" now shows only when Pathfinder is certain: you're in the world as the character it tracks, and your log is silent.

- Patching, login, server select and character select no longer set it off. EverQuest writes nothing to your log until you're in the world, and Pathfinder used to warn after 3 minutes whatever you were doing.
- It reads the game's own debug log to see who logged in, on which server, and when the zone finished loading. A minute after that, with nothing in your log, it tells you once.
- Playing another character, or the same name on another server, never sets it off.
- No re-read.
