Trios are now checked against the spells you cast. The log never says when you swap classes, so Pathfinder used to carry your last /who for days; now it notices a swap from what you cast and works out the new trio (hover a trio in Crawls to see how it's known). Relevels now count from their very first run. The first start after this update re-reads your logs, which takes a few minutes. Fixed: the Where and Who with switches on Ask now change the answer. Beta: Ask shows numbers from players who share beside your own (dungeons and instances for now).
