Upgrades names motes when condensing; Tidier number boxes; Upgrades plans merging your duplicate items.

Upgrades: the Condense step now says motes ("2 Greater motes → 1 Superior mote"), so it can't be read as fragments.

- Only motes condense at Constructed Potential; fragments can't.

Number boxes no longer squeeze their arrows against the number.

- Your motes: the boxes drop the arrows. Clicking one selects its count, so you can type the new one straight over it. Up and Down still step it.
- Everywhere else the arrows sit a little apart from the number.

Upgrades: when you have more than one copy of an item, it shows what merging them saves and plans the cheapest way to do it.

- Other copies are filled in from your inventory export; type +N for any it's missing.
- Keep both, or Merge them in: the plan raises each copy on the cheapest motes that fit, then merges them. Two copies at +5 raised to +7 and merged make +8 for about half the cost of raising one copy alone.
- Either way, a tile shows what merging saves.
- No re-read.
