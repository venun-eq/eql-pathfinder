Dying isn't a class change; Races you want first; Campaign goals beside your runs; A combo to try; Your runs as a table by round.

Dying no longer makes Pathfinder think you changed a class.

- When you died and respawned in the same zone, the overlay said you'd changed a class on your loadout and asked for a /who. It now knows a death from a class change, and keeps comparing you with your usual.

In the new planner (beta), a race can be a goal without being ranked, as a class can.

- Everything 3/3 now makes every race a goal and ranks none for you: races you ranked stay ranked, and Pathfinder places the rest the cheapest way. With nothing ranked, the runs and the XP to level are the same as before.
- The race tile's pips are buttons now: click one for the rank you want, click your goal's pip again to lower it.
- Races you want first works like Classes you want first: click a race's chip to rank it, and Leave it to Pathfinder takes it out of the list and keeps its goal.
- If your race list held all 16 races at 3/3, none set to Unlock now (as Everything 3/3 left it), it empties once, with a line saying so. Your goals and runs stay. Rank a race again to put it first.

Campaign puts your goals beside your runs, so you see your plan without scrolling past its settings. Everyone gets it, with or without the beta planner.

- The header is one slim band; Your goals sit in a column beside the runs, and stack above them in a narrow window.
- The Cheapest path card is gone. Its words now sit behind the (i) at the end of the totals line, and the line itself only speaks up when your plan takes more runs than it needs.
- Three ready-made goals sit on top of Your goals: Everything 3/3, Every class 3/3 and Clear goals, each with the runs it would take from here. A click keeps your pinned runs, the runs you played and, on the new planner, your combos. Every race 3/3 is now set on the race pips: there is no separate toggle for it any more.
- Classes and races are twin tiles of pips: click a pip for the rank you want, click it again to lower it. One ring marks your goal; a goal the plan misses gets a dashed red one. A legend names every mark on the page.
- Under each tile, how many of the ranks you want you have, and by which run the plan gets the rest.

In the new planner (beta), an empty Your combos offers one combo to try.

- It's the trio you've played most, when you've played one twice or more, else two classes already together in your next run. Either way it's a Prefer, so it costs nothing.
- Add this opens Add a combo filled in, with what it does. Nothing goes in your plan until you add it.
- A new plan still starts with no combos.

Campaign's Your runs is now a table by round: one short line per loadout, so a long plan reads at a glance.

- Each line has the run number, the race, three classes and the XP to level, with three pips on every name for its rank: solid gold you have it, dark gold this plan earns it, empty not yet. A key under the intro says so.
- Runs are grouped under a header that says what the round does: Played, Leveling now, Round 1, 2 and 3 (classes to 1/3, 2/3, 3/3, with the runs, the XP to level and where Completionist rises) and Races. Every group still to come has Fold, so a round you don't need stays out of the way.
- A name is gold on the line that takes it to 3/3 and grey where it is 3/3 already, so a run that only adds XP to level shows without a warning.
- Swap… opens under its line and starts with what the run earns, then the cheapest changes, as before.
- The "Two pets" warning is gone. You chose the trio; Pathfinder no longer second-guesses it.
- Everyone gets it, with or without the beta planner.
- No re-read.
