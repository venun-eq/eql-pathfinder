Fight meter: melee skills get their own rows. Backstab, Kick, Bash, Frenzy, Strike, Cleave, Smite and Reave (and Archery) show separately from Melee, which is now auto-attack only, for you and your groupmates. The meter re-reads your fight history once after this update (about a minute), so your usual splits the same way, and the phantom extra mob from Frenzy ('Lord of Ire +1') is cleared from past fights too.
