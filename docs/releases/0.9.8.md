New: the fight meter, a second overlay with damage per second by fighter, spell, mob or fight, against your usual in the same place, plus healing and damage taken. Your pets count as you (click your row for the split). Switch between active time and kill time from its footer. Overlays: the crawl overlay and the fight meter now work the same way, with one Overlays menu on the tray compass and one Overlays card in Settings. Double-click the compass to lock or unlock both; Arrange overlays shows each one with its name so you can place them. The first start reads your past fights once, about 15 seconds.
