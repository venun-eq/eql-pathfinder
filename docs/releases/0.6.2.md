Welcome tour, reincarnations from before your log, and a warning when your log is off.

- A short welcome tour: what Pathfinder does, turning your log on (it checks it live), reincarnations from before your log, the overlay and your goal. You'll see it once; Help → Show the welcome tour brings it back.
- Reincarnated while your log was off? Roster → Set counts. Your log's later reincarnations add on by themselves.
- If EverQuest is running but your log isn't being written, the title bar, overlay and tray say "type /log on".
- Pathfinder re-reads your history once after this update (about a minute).
