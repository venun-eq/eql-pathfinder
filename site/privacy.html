Privacy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Pathfinder runs on your PC and reads your EverQuest Legends log there. Your log, your character's name and your chat never leave your PC. Three things can: anonymous usage counts if you say yes to them, your play's numbers if you choose to share (off until you turn it on), and feedback and reports you send. You can see each one before it goes, and stop or delete it from the app.

What stays on your PC

Pathfinder reads the log the game writes when you type /log on, the game's spell list, and your /outputfile inventory export. It never changes the game, its files or its memory. What it works out from them (your crawls, kills, levels, motes, fights and settings) is kept in %LocalAppData%\EQL Pathfinder. Uninstalling Pathfinder from Windows Settings removes the app; delete that folder too to remove what it kept.

Anonymous usage counts

Pathfinder asks when you set it up, and sends nothing until you say yes. Usage counts let us see how many copies are in use, which versions, which pages earn their place, and what crashes. Change your answer any time in Settings → Character and data → Send anonymous usage counts; switching it off stops the counting and deletes everything your copy sent from the server. If the server can't be reached then, Pathfinder asks again until it's done. Copies installed before the question was added had them on by default and keep the setting they have. See exactly what's sent in the same place shows today's summary as it would go now.

One summary per day, sent a couple of minutes after Pathfinder starts, hourly while anything changed, and when it closes. Each summary carries:

  • A random id made for usage counts only (not your sharing key, not your feedback id), so counts can't be joined to anything else you send.
  • The day, the app version, stable or beta, your Windows version, and the day this copy first counted.
  • Counts: pages opened, Ask questions asked, which features you used and which notices you answered, setup and tour steps reached, how often Pathfinder started or updated, minutes it ran with and without the game, and how long reading your logs took and how big they were (a number of megabytes, nothing from inside them).
  • Which settings were on: sharing, the beta, overlays, start window, goal, and whether Pathfinder updates itself.
  • Errors: what Pathfinder was doing, the kind of error and where in Pathfinder it happened, never the error message (messages can hold file paths or names).

Never sent: log lines, character or server names, your play's numbers, file paths, or times of day. The time a summary arrives isn't kept, only its day. Summaries are deleted after 400 days.

Sharing (optional)

Off until you turn it on in Settings → Sharing. When it's on, Pathfinder sends the numbers each crawl and hunt is made of, every 10 minutes, so Ask can show how fast other players level and farm where you haven't been. See what's sent shows the exact data before anything goes.

SentNever sent
For each crawl or hunt: the zone and difficulty, levels, the trio, race and reincarnation rank, each kill's seconds into the crawl, XP and kill time, when you stepped out of the crawl and came back and when it ended (also as seconds into it), solo or grouped, whether it was during a bonus-XP event (those are left out of the community numbers), deaths, damage per spell or ability, and motes and fragments by tier. The day and an order instead of times. The game server's name. Your character's name (a code made with a random key on your PC stands in, and can't be turned back into the name). Chat, tells, guild and group messages, other players' names. Mob and item names other than motes and fragments. Times of day, log files and folders.

What everyone shares becomes the community numbers, rebuilt every 30 minutes and published for every copy of Pathfinder to read. They are totals per place, level and difficulty: each one needs several players and crawls behind it, and no one player can outweigh the others.

Pause stops sending and keeps what you shared. Delete everything I shared removes it all from the server at once. Shared rows are deleted after a year in any case.

Each copy that shares signs its uploads with a key made on your PC and stored encrypted for your Windows account. The key is what lets only your copy add to or delete your data; no account or email is involved.

Feedback, reports and votes

Only when you send them, from Help or Report. Feedback carries what you wrote, its kind, the app version and page, a name only if you give one, and Pathfinder's error log only if you tick the box. Votes on what's coming carry an anonymous id for your copy, so each copy votes once; take a vote back and it's gone. Feedback is kept so we can act on it.

A report from Report also carries your window's size and display scaling and your versions of Windows and of Microsoft's WebView2, and, unless you untick it, a screenshot. The screenshot shows Pathfinder's own page only: never your desktop, the game or other windows. Your character's name, clock times and folders are hidden before it's taken, you see it before it's sent, and anything you black out is covered for good. Screenshots are kept privately for 90 days, then deleted.

Feedback and reports carry the same anonymous id as votes, so Help → Your reports can show what your copy sent and what happened to it.

Updates and community numbers

Pathfinder checks updates.eqlpathfinder.com for new versions and downloads them, and every copy downloads the community numbers from the same place every 30 minutes. These requests carry the app version and nothing about you or your play.

Your internet address

Pathfinder's server doesn't store addresses. It sees yours while answering a request, to slow down anyone hammering it, and then forgets it. For sharing, feedback, reports and votes, it keeps a code made from your address, the day and a secret key. With it, only one new player a day can start sharing from the same connection, sharing stays at one character per game server per connection (the game allows one character per server), and one connection can send only so much feedback and so many reports, screenshots and votes in a day. The code can't be turned back into your address and is deleted after two days.

Where it's kept

The server, its database and the update files run on Cloudflare, which carries every request to them. Cloudflare's handling of the traffic it carries is covered by its own privacy policy. Pathfinder doesn't sell or hand over what it receives, and uses it only to run and improve Pathfinder.

This website

No cookies, no scripts, no analytics, and nothing loaded from anywhere else.

Changes and questions

When this page changes, the date at the top changes too, and a change to what Pathfinder sends is announced in the app and in What's new. Questions, or a request about your data: use Help → Send feedback in the app.
