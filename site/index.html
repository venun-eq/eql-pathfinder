Ask

Where should I level next?

Pick a level range and Ask lays out the fastest route, place by place, timed from your own runs and adjusted for the extra XP each reincarnation needs.

Turn on Count motes too and a slower place can win when its motes are worth the time. Ask also answers:

  • The best mote farm at your level
  • Which difficulty is worth it
  • Whether your damage is normal for the place
While you play

Know if this run is on pace.

A small overlay shows levels per hour, kill speed and XP per kill against your usual at this place and level. A tick marks your usual, and when you fall behind it says whether it's you or the place.

The fight meter splits damage by fighter, spell and mob for each pull, crawl or session, against your usual in the same place. Your pets count as you.

The overlay and the fight meter sit over the game. Drag them where you like, or hide them.

Relevels

Race your best relevel.

Every relevel is a line on the race chart and a bar of where its hours went, with splits and kill speed. Times are put on the same footing, so a relevel at more XP per level still compares fairly.

Campaign lays out every reincarnation toward the passive ranks you want, ticking runs off as your log sees them.

History

Every visit, measured.

Each crawl, raid and open-world hunt with its time, levels per hour, T5-eq, kills, deaths and a verdict against your usual there: on pace or slow while you level, more or less T5-eq an hour while you farm. Loot keeps every mote and fragment by tier, with drop rates per kill and per crawl.

Upgrades works out the cheapest way to raise an item or spell with the motes and fragments you have.

Optional

Better together.

Where your own logs are thin, Ask answers from players who share: how fast they level and farm in places you haven't run, and how many players stand behind each number.

Share your play and it fills in theirs. Only numbers go: levels, times, kills, motes and damage. Never names, chat or clock times. Sharing is off until you turn it on in Settings.

Trust

It reads. It never touches the game.

Only reads

Pathfinder reads the log the game writes when you type /log on, the game's spell list, and your /outputfile inventory export. It never changes the game, its files or its memory.

Stays on your PC

What it reads and works out stays on your PC unless you choose to share. Anonymous usage counts are sent only if you say yes, and you can see exactly what's sent.

Signed and current

The installer and the app are signed with a publisher Microsoft has verified. New versions arrive by themselves.

Open source

All of Pathfinder's code is on GitHub under the MIT license. Read what it reads and sends, or build it yourself.

Get started

Three steps, about five minutes.

  1. InstallDownload and run the installer. It installs for your Windows account only; no admin rights needed.
  2. Turn your log onIn EverQuest Legends, type /log on. The welcome tour checks that it's working.
  3. PlayPathfinder reads your history once (a few minutes for a big log), then keeps up as you play.
Installing

Windows may warn you the first time.

Windows can show this blue box for a new app until enough people have installed it. It's about how new Pathfinder is, not about what's in it.

  1. Click More info.
  2. Check the publisher: a name Microsoft has verified, not Unknown publisher. It's the developer's own name; Pathfinder is published as Venun.
  3. Click Run anyway.

Rather look first? Pathfinder's source is public on GitHub: what it reads, what it sends, and how to build it yourself.
