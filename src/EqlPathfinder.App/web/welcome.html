It only reads your log file. It never touches the game or its memory, and your play stays on this PC unless you choose to share it.
Usage counts

Send anonymous usage counts?

Once a day, a short summary of how Pathfinder ran: pages opened, minutes, errors. It shows what to fix first. Nothing is sent unless you say yes.

In the summary
  • Its own random id, not your sharing key
  • The date, Pathfinder and Windows versions, and the day this copy first counted
  • Pages opened, Ask questions, features used, notices answered
  • Setup steps, starts and updates
  • Settings: sharing, beta, overlays, start window, goal, self-update
  • Minutes run, with and without the game
  • How long your log took to read, and its size
  • Errors: their kind and where, never the message
Never in it
  • Your log, or any line from it
  • Character, server or guild names
  • Chat, your kills, levels or loot, file paths
  • The time of day: only the date
  • Your IP address: not kept
